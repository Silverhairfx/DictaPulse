# Code of Conduct

I want DictaPulse to be a useful, respectful project for people who care about private voice dictation on Linux and, over time, other platforms. Good technical discussion is welcome here. Personal attacks, harassment, and bad-faith behavior are not.

## Our Standards

Please help keep the project healthy by:

- Being respectful of different experience levels, backgrounds, languages, and communication styles.
- Giving feedback that focuses on the work, not the person.
- Opening issues with enough detail for someone else to reproduce or understand the problem.
- Staying patient when a maintainer decision is made to protect project quality, user safety, or community health.
- Giving proper credit to contributors and upstream projects.

Behavior that is not welcome:

- Harassment, intimidation, insults, or personal attacks.
- Sexualized language or imagery, unwanted sexual attention, or inappropriate contact.
- Discriminatory comments related to identity, background, ability, language, religion, nationality, or similar characteristics.
- Trolling, sustained derailment, or repeatedly ignoring maintainer guidance.
- Publishing private information without explicit permission.
- Pressuring maintainers or contributors for unpaid work, timelines, private support, or off-channel access.
- Using project spaces to promote malware, spyware, credential theft, license violations, or closed-source redistribution that violates DictaPulse's GPL license.

## Scope

This applies anywhere people are participating in the DictaPulse project: GitHub issues, pull requests, discussions, releases, community spaces, and private maintainer conversations related to the project.

Use the subject line `DictaPulse Code of Conduct report`.

Please include:

- What happened.
- Where it happened.
- Links, screenshots, or message IDs if available.
- Whether there is an immediate safety concern.

Reports are handled privately. Maintainers will only share details with people who need them in order to review and respond.

## Enforcement

I may take action to protect the project and the people participating in it. That can include:

- Clarifying expectations in private or in public.
- Editing or removing comments.
- Locking threads.
- Rejecting or closing issues and pull requests.
- Temporarily or permanently blocking a person from project spaces.
- Reporting serious abuse to GitHub or other relevant platform operators.

Enforcement is based on the impact of the behavior, not only the intent.

## Maintainer Responsibilities

Maintainers are expected to apply this Code of Conduct fairly. Contributions may be removed or rejected if they do not align with this policy, even when the technical work is useful.
